# Bing AI Citation Report Reader

## What this skill does

A reading protocol for Bing's AI reporting: where to find cited-page data, how to interpret citation counts against the owned asset list, and how to reconcile Bing's numbers with the programme's own Copilot tracker slice when they disagree.

Category: **Monitoring & Measurement** — Prove it monthly: frozen prompt sets, citation logs, scorecards and regression alerts — the discipline that separates AEO from superstition.

## Inputs

- Bing Webmaster Tools access for the verified property
- The citable asset list

## Workflow

1. **Locate the AI citation views** — Open the AI performance/citation reports; note the date range and that counts lag real time — record the window with every reading.
2. **List cited pages** — Export/notate every owned page cited, with counts. Compare against the asset list: cited assets, and assets that should be cited but are not.
3. **Investigate the zeros** — For each uncited key asset: indexed in Bing? robots/Copilot access fine? The fix is usually indexation, per copilot-bing-citation-tactics.
4. **Reconcile with the tracker** — Where Bing's data and the monthly Copilot run disagree, trust the saved answer evidence for facts and Bing for trend; log the discrepancy rather than hiding it.
5. **Report the reading** — One short section in the monthly scorecard: Bing-cited pages, new entrants, zeros fixed.

## Output

A monthly Bing AI reading (cited pages, zeros + fixes, discrepancies logged).

## Measurement

Key-asset zero-citation list shrinks; every monthly scorecard carries a Bing reading with its date window stated.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Vendor dashboards are not ground truth. Report their figures as the platform's counts, with windows, next to your own evidence-based tracker.

Fictional: Bing reports the flagship page cited 40+ times while two newer articles sit at zero — both found unindexed in Bing and fixed the same week.
