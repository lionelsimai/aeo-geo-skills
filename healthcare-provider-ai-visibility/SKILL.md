# Healthcare Provider AI Visibility

## What this skill does

Applies AEO to healthcare with the safety bar raised: services and practitioner facts stated precisely, content authored/reviewed by qualified clinicians, and no treatment claims beyond approved, evidence-based wording. Visibility never outruns medical accuracy or local healthcare advertising rules.

Category: **Local & Vertical AEO** — The five layers applied per vertical: local businesses, B2B SaaS, ecommerce, professional services and more — same discipline, different surfaces.

## Inputs

- Practitioner credentials and registered specialisms
- Approved service descriptions (per local healthcare advertising regulation)
- Clinic fact sheet (locations, hours, contact)

## Workflow

1. **Freeze clinical facts** — Services, conditions treated and practitioner registrations exactly as licensed. Marketing drafts nothing clinical without clinician sign-off.
2. **Author under credentials** — Health explainers carry the reviewing clinician's name and registration; generic content-farm health text is prohibited.
3. **State limits honestly** — Content says what a service can and cannot do, and when to seek urgent care where relevant. Engines citing you must not become a misinformation channel.
4. **Align the local base** — Maps/GBP, directories and insurer panels carry identical clinic facts (local-business-ai-visibility rules).
5. **Track informational prompts** — 'What does [procedure] involve?', '[specialist] in [place]' — checking answers for medical accuracy, not just mentions.

## Output

A healthcare visibility pack (facts frozen, credentialed content pipeline, local alignment) + accuracy-checked prompt tracking.

## Measurement

Mention rate on service prompts AND factual accuracy of health answers (both reported; accuracy gates any celebration of visibility).

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Never promise cures, publish patient stories without consent, or let AI draft clinical claims unreviewed. A single medical misinformation incident ends the programme and possibly the licence.
