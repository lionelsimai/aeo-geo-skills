# Data Landing Page Builder

## What this skill does

Turns a report from a citable-nothing PDF into a citation magnet: a permanent URL whose first screen is the headline statistics as standalone units, with methodology, edition history and the full report one click down.

Category: **Citable Content** — Layer 2 of the strategy: pages built to be lifted — answer-first structure, standalone numbers, FAQ blocks, original data.

## Inputs

- The report/dataset and its verified headline figures (from quotable-stat-extractor)
- Methodology summary
- Prior editions, if any

## Workflow

1. **Lead with the numbers** — The first screen: 4-6 headline stats as stat units with source and date clauses. This screen is what engines cite.
2. **Show the methodology** — Sample, method, period and limitations in plain language on the same page. Hidden methods read as marketing.
3. **Structure the findings** — One H2 per finding, each opening with its key figure, then interpretation. Every finding is a liftable unit.
4. **Archive editions visibly** — Edition number and year in the URL strategy: one permanent page, prior editions linked. Never delete an old edition engines cite.
5. **Add the citation line** — 'Cite as: [Org], [Report] [Edition] ([Year]).' with the permanent URL. Make correct citation effortless.

## Output

A landing page spec (stats screen, findings structure, methodology block, citation line) for the flagship asset.

## Measurement

Citations of the landing page (not the PDF) in AI answers; year-on-year citation growth per edition.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Do not gate the headline stats behind the download form. What engines cannot read, they cannot cite.
