# Podcast Pitch & Prep

## What this skill does

Podcasts corroborate twice: the episode page (a third-party surface carrying the bio) and the transcript (retrievable text). This skill runs both sides — a fit-based pitch, and a prep pack that gives the show the canonical bio and credit line to publish.

Category: **Third-Party Corroboration** — Layer 3 of the strategy: independent sources describing the entity the same way — the layer that actually moves prize queries.

## Inputs

- Target shows (audience fit over download claims)
- Canonical bio pack and quote bank
- 3 topic angles the guest can genuinely carry

## Workflow

1. **Select by audience fit** — Shows the target audience actually hears, in the entity's niche and geography. Vanity metrics from the show are not evidence.
2. **Pitch one angle per show** — Reference a recent episode, propose a specific topic and what the audience takes away. Generic 'I'd love to chat' pitches are deleted.
3. **Send the bio pack on booking** — Canonical bio (50-word), exact descriptor, headshot, links — before the show writes its own version from an old page.
4. **Prep the talking points** — 3-5 points, each with a stat or story the guest can evidence. Note the phrases to use for the entity's own descriptor and organisation.
5. **Close the loop after airing** — Confirm the episode page carries the right bio and links; share the episode; log the transcript URL as a citation surface.

## Output

A podcast pipeline (targets, angle, status) and a per-appearance prep pack.

## Measurement

Appearances per quarter on fitted shows; % of episode pages carrying the canonical bio and correct descriptor.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Never pay for undisclosed 'guest placements' on podcast mills. The corroboration value of a bought slot is negative once noticed.
