# Media Kit Builder

## What this skill does

One link that answers every journalist/producer request: canonical bios (all lengths), the credit line brief, verified proof stats, downloadable headshots, past-coverage links, and contact. The kit's job is accuracy at speed — every asset engineered so the correct descriptor is the path of least resistance.

Category: **Brand & Expert Positioning** — The person as an entity: positioning statements, speaking and book evidence, media kits and identity-answer repair.

## Inputs

- Canonical bio pack + credit line brief
- Stat bank (verified figures only)
- Headshots/logo at publication resolution, past coverage links

## Workflow

1. **Lead with the credit line** — The kit opens with the exact credit line and one-liner — before downloads, before design.
2. **Include all bio lengths** — One-liner, 50, 150 and 300-word versions, copy-paste ready as plain text (not locked in a PDF image).
3. **Publish the proof stats** — The top 5 verified stats with sources and dates, from the stat bank — journalists lift these into pieces, which engines then cite.
4. **Add coverage and assets** — Best past coverage linked (with the descriptor visible in those pieces), headshots in two crops, logo files.
5. **Keep it alive** — The kit is a page on the owned site, updated on the same triggers as the bio pack; a stale PDF kit circulating by email is a defect.

## Output

A live media-kit page (bios, credit line, stats, assets, coverage, contact).

## Measurement

Coverage produced from the kit carries the correct descriptor without correction requests; kit facts re-verified quarterly.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Do not include audience/reach numbers that platforms contradict. A journalist who catches one inflated figure discards the whole kit.
