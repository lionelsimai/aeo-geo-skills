# Digital PR Story Angler

## What this skill does

Develops story angles from what journalists actually need: a data hook from original research, a sharp expert position on live news, or a counter-intuitive finding — packaged with the quote bank and credit line for target outlets.

Category: **Third-Party Corroboration** — Layer 3 of the strategy: independent sources describing the entity the same way — the layer that actually moves prize queries.

## Inputs

- Original data or a genuinely expert position
- Target outlet list (trade press, national media, vertical publications)
- The expert's availability and quote bank

## Workflow

1. **Find the news hook** — Tie the angle to something already moving: a report launch, a policy change, a market event. Cold 'we're interesting' pitches die.
2. **Lead with the data or the line** — The pitch's first two lines carry the strongest stat or quote. Journalists triage in seconds.
3. **Offer the expert properly** — Name, exact descriptor credit line, one-line proof of expertise, fast availability. Make quoting effortless and attribution correct.
4. **Target precisely** — One angle per outlet beat, sent to the journalist who covers it. Mass blasts are logged as spam by the people you need.
5. **Convert the placement** — When quoted, syndicate correctly: owned article expanding the quote, links, and the quote added to the bank with its source.

## Output

An angle sheet (hook, lead stat/quote, target outlets, pitch status) per campaign.

## Measurement

Quoted placements in target-class outlets per month (the strategy's cadence: one quality quote beats ten posts), each carrying the correct descriptor.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Do not manufacture a 'study' as a PR prop with a junk sample. One exposed prop ends an expert's media credibility.

## Fictional example

Fictional: a community dataset on AI adoption becomes a trade-press story; the founder is quoted with the exact credit line 'agentic AI expert and founder of…' — the descriptor engines then repeat.
