# Repurpose Talks into Articles

## What this skill does

The compounding move from the strategy: every appearance becomes an owned article. The talk transcript is the draft — restructured answer-first, claims verified against the fact allow-list, published on the owned site with the video/recording embedded and linked.

Category: **GEO Content Ops & Workflows** — Run AEO as an operation: freshness systems, briefs, calendars, QA gates and agency delivery — the workflows that keep citations compounding.

## Inputs

- Talk/podcast recording or transcript
- The appearance's event page (for the corroboration register)
- Fact allow-list for verification

## Workflow

1. **Get the transcript** — From the recording or platform. Clean it for readability without changing meaning; mark any passage that is unclear rather than guessing it.
2. **Restructure answer-first** — The article opens with the talk's core answer in 100 words, then sections by the talk's questions — not a chronological transcript dump.
3. **Verify every claim** — Stats and claims in the talk get checked against sources before print. A spoken approximation becomes a written fact only if it verifies.
4. **Publish with the media** — Article embeds/links the recording; the video description links back to the article. Both carry the canonical bio.
5. **Log both assets** — Article joins the citable inventory; the appearance page joins the corroboration register. One effort, two evidence layers.

## Output

A published article per appearance + register/citation-log entries for both assets.

## Measurement

Appearances converted to articles (% target 100%); articles from talks earning citations (talks usually outperform cold articles — verify in the log).

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Never publish a transcript's errors as the expert's words. Clean, verify, and where the spoken claim was wrong, the article prints the correct fact.
