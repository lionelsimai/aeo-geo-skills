# ChatGPT Citation Tactics

## What this skill does

Works ChatGPT's behaviour empirically: run the prompt set, study which sources it retrieves and cites (heavily web-search driven, roundup- and reference-friendly), and align the entity's corroboration and citable assets to those observed preferences.

Category: **Platform Surfaces** — Layer 4 of the strategy: each engine retrieves and cites differently — per-engine tactics grounded in observed citation behaviour.

## Inputs

- The frozen prompt set and a ChatGPT audit run
- Citation-source audit for ChatGPT specifically

## Workflow

1. **Observe before theorising** — From saved answers: which domains does ChatGPT cite for your prompts, and in what order? Work from the sample, not from blog folklore.
2. **Note the roundup weight** — ChatGPT leans on listicles and reference pages for 'best/top' prompts — corroboration targets follow from your sample's evidence.
3. **Check entity resolution** — Ask 'Who is X?' and follow-ups. If ChatGPT hedges or uses stale facts, the fix is entity-layer, not content volume.
4. **Feed the retrieval layer** — Owned pages must be indexable and ranking in web search; ChatGPT cannot cite what its retrieval cannot find.
5. **Re-run on the monthly cadence** — Same prompts, logged answers. ChatGPT's source mix shifts with product updates — the log catches drift early.

## Output

A ChatGPT behaviour note (observed source preferences for this category) + the aligned corroboration/content actions.

## Measurement

Mention and citation rates on the ChatGPT slice of the monthly tracker, reported per engine — never blended into a cross-engine average.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: One good answer in your own logged-in session proves nothing. Use fresh sessions and the frozen set; personalisation flatters.

Fictional: across 12 prompts ChatGPT cites roundups in 9 answers; the programme shifts effort from new articles to roundup inclusion for a quarter.
