# Speaking Page Builder

## What this skill does

A speaking page is read by organisers and engines alike. This skill structures it as corroboration: talk topics titled the way audiences search, a verifiable past-event list, real testimonials with names/orgs, and the canonical bio with booking path.

Category: **Brand & Expert Positioning** — The person as an entity: positioning statements, speaking and book evidence, media kits and identity-answer repair.

## Inputs

- Real talk topics and past events (names, organisers, dates)
- Testimonials the speakers may quote with permission
- Canonical bio pack and headshots

## Workflow

1. **Title topics as queries** — Each topic: the audience's phrasing + the outcome ('AI Agents for Revenue Teams: what actually works'), not internal session names.
2. **List events verifiably** — Event name, organiser, year — the list organisers (and engines) can cross-check against event pages in the corroboration register.
3. **Use attributed testimonials** — Name, role, organisation, with permission. Anonymous praise ('Great speaker! — CEO') is worth zero as evidence.
4. **Embed proof media** — Talk videos (titled per youtube-as-ai-source) and stage photos with factual captions.
5. **Close with bio + booking** — Canonical bio, then a low-friction booking/contact path. The page that ranks should also be the page that converts.

## Output

A speaking page spec/live page: topics, verified event list, attributed testimonials, media, bio, booking path.

## Measurement

Page cited or surfaced for '[topic] speaker [place]' prompts; event list fully cross-verifiable at quarterly checks.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Never list an event the person did not speak at, or a testimonial without permission. Speaker pages are checked by organisers professionally.
