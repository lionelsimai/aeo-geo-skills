# Review Platform Presence Builder

## What this skill does

Sets up and maintains review profiles honestly: claim the profile, complete it with canonical facts, invite reviews from real customers at natural moments, and respond professionally. Review platforms are heavily cited by engines for product/service prompts.

Category: **Third-Party Corroboration** — Layer 3 of the strategy: independent sources describing the entity the same way — the layer that actually moves prize queries.

## Inputs

- The platforms where the category is actually reviewed
- Real customer relationships (the only legitimate review source)
- Canonical fact sheet for profile copy

## Workflow

1. **Claim and complete profiles** — Canonical name, category, description and links. Wrong category placement is the most common silent failure.
2. **Build the invitation habit** — Ask real customers at moments of delivered value, with a direct link. Timing and ease are legitimate; incentives are not.
3. **Never gate or fake** — No review gating (only asking the happy), no staff/family reviews, no purchased reviews. Platforms and engines both detect and punish it.
4. **Respond to everything** — Thank positives specifically; answer negatives factually with the fix. Responses are read by engines and buyers alike.
5. **Surface the aggregate honestly** — Where the site cites a rating, it matches the platform's live figure. A rounded-up rating on your site is a fabrication.

## Output

Completed platform profiles, an invitation process doc, and a response routine.

## Measurement

Review count/recency and rating as shown on the platforms themselves; profile facts 100% canonical at quarterly checks.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: A single fake-review scandal outranks years of good reviews in answers. The policy is absolute: real customers only.
