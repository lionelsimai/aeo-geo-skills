# Team Training Playbook (AEO)

## What this skill does

Turns the whole collection into a training path: the three-step model (resolve entity, check corroboration, lift citable units), the five layers, the monthly routines, and the white-hat red lines — taught with the team's own baseline as the case study, in short sessions with exercises.

Category: **GEO Content Ops & Workflows** — Run AEO as an operation: freshness systems, briefs, calendars, QA gates and agency delivery — the workflows that keep citations compounding.

## Inputs

- The team's current baseline and artefacts (real examples teach better than slides)
- Roles to train (writers, PR, web, leads)

## Workflow

1. **Teach the model first** — One session: how engines resolve, corroborate and lift. Every later routine is taught as serving one of the three steps.
2. **Train by role** — Writers: answer-first + briefs + QA. PR/outreach: corroboration rules + credit lines. Web: schema, llms.txt, indexation floor. Leads: tracker + scorecard reading.
3. **Practise on live artefacts** — Exercises use the team's real pages/prompts: rewrite an opening answer-first, diff a bio set, score a run. No toy examples.
4. **Install the red lines** — No fake citations/stats, no spam placements, no guarantees, claims via allow-lists only — recited, written into the playbook, and enforced at QA.
5. **Certify by doing** — Each trainee ships one supervised artefact (a brief passed QA, a completed run, a verified sweep) before working unsupervised.

## Output

A training playbook (sessions, exercises, red lines) + per-role completion records.

## Measurement

Trainees shipping gate-passing artefacts unsupervised within one cycle; QA defect rates by cohort improving quarter on quarter.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Do not train tactics without the model. A team that knows 'add FAQ schema' but not why engines lift answers will cargo-cult the collection into spam.
