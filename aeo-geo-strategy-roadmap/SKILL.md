# AEO/GEO Strategy Roadmap

## What this skill does

Builds the programme plan: what happens in weeks 1-2 (entity + technical floor), weeks 2-8 (citable content), weeks 2-12 (corroboration), and the monthly measurement loop — each item with an owner and a done-definition.

Category: **AEO/GEO Strategy & Audits** — Know where the brand stands in AI answers before spending anything: audits, baselines, share of voice and roadmaps.

## Inputs

- Completed audit, baseline and readiness scorecard
- Who can publish, pitch and approve (names/roles)
- The entity's existing assets (reports, talks, books, data)

## Workflow

1. **Sequence by dependency** — Entity foundation first — corroboration pitched before the bio is consistent wastes the pitch. Technical floor in the same first pass.
2. **Assign flagship content** — Name the one flagship citable asset (original data or definitive guide) and its landing page; schedule query articles at a sustainable cadence (2/month beats 10 once).
3. **Build the corroboration pipeline** — Roundup pitches, one press quote target per month, podcast circuit — each with the canonical bio/credit line attached.
4. **Set measurement gates** — Day 30: Tier 3 fixed. Day 60: first Tier 2 citations. Day 90: full re-audit vs baseline. Write these into the plan.
5. **List what NOT to do** — Name the skipped tactics explicitly (spam listicles, volume blogging, premature Wikipedia) so the plan is not 'improved' into them later.

## Output

A 90-day roadmap document: phased actions, owners, done-definitions, measurement gates, and the explicit skip list.

## Measurement

Gate reviews at days 30/60/90 against the baseline. A roadmap item counts as done only when it is live and indexable, not drafted.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Never promise Tier 1 placement dates. Promise the gates you control (publishing, pitching, measurement) and report placement as an outcome, not a deliverable.
