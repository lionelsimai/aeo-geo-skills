# Editorial Calendar for AI Citations

## What this skill does

Rebuilds the editorial calendar around the tier map: each planned piece names its target prompt, the format that wins it, the facts it needs, and its measurement date. Cadence is deliberately modest — two strong citable pieces a month beat a daily blog nobody cites.

Category: **GEO Content Ops & Workflows** — Run AEO as an operation: freshness systems, briefs, calendars, QA gates and agency delivery — the workflows that keep citations compounding.

## Inputs

- Tier map + citation gap planner output
- Production capacity (writers, approvers)
- Seasonal moments the audience actually has (budget cycles, admissions, peak seasons)

## Workflow

1. **Slot by gap, not by whim** — Calendar entries are drawn from the citation-driven gap list in priority order; unprompted topics wait.
2. **Assign format per prompt** — Definition, comparison, guide, data update — the format the source audit shows winning for that prompt type.
3. **Schedule facts before drafts** — Each entry lists the data/interviews it needs and when they are secured; a slot without its facts secured is moved, not bluffed.
4. **Build in refresh slots** — One slot per month is a refresh of a cited page (freshness system), so maintenance is planned, not heroic.
5. **Review against citations quarterly** — The calendar is judged by citation log movement, and next quarter's slots are drawn from what the log proved.

## Output

A quarterly calendar (piece, prompt, format, facts needed, publish date, measurement date).

## Measurement

Planned-vs-published rate; % of new pieces earning a citation within 2 runs (tracked honestly, expected to start low).

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Do not let the calendar refill with easy opinion posts when data pieces slip. Opinion volume is how AEO programmes quietly become blogs.
