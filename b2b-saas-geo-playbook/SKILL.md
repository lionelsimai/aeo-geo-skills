# B2B SaaS GEO Playbook

## What this skill does

Adapts the five layers to how B2B software is researched in AI answers: category and use-case prompts, 'X vs Y' and 'alternatives to X' pages, G2/Capterra-class review presence, docs/help content as citable sources, and analyst/community corroboration.

Category: **Local & Vertical AEO** — The five layers applied per vertical: local businesses, B2B SaaS, ecommerce, professional services and more — same discipline, different surfaces.

## Inputs

- Product category and use cases as buyers phrase them
- Review platform status
- Docs/help-centre inventory

## Workflow

1. **Map the prompt families** — Category ('best [category] for [segment]'), use-case ('tool to do X'), comparison and alternatives prompts — each family needs a different asset.
2. **Build comparison/alternatives assets** — Honest comparison pages (comparison-page-builder) including for competitors' 'alternatives' prompts, with sourced specs.
3. **Win the review platforms** — Claimed, correctly-categorised profiles and a real-customer review habit; these dominate SaaS citation patterns.
4. **Make docs citable** — Help/docs pages answer-first with the product's real capabilities and limits; engines cite docs for how-to and capability prompts.
5. **Track per family** — Monthly tracker split by prompt family, so a comparison weakness is not hidden by category strength.

## Output

A SaaS GEO plan (prompt families x assets) + the first comparison and docs fixes shipped.

## Measurement

Citation rate per prompt family; review-platform presence current; docs pages appearing in capability-prompt citations.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Never seed fake G2/Capterra reviews or fake comparison specs. SaaS buyers and platforms police this aggressively, and engines retain the fallout.
