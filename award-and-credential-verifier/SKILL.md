# Award & Credential Verifier

## What this skill does

Audits every award, certification and credential the entity claims, and rebuilds their presentation so each one is checkable in one click: issuing body, year, credential/record link. Anything unverifiable is removed — an exposed fake credential destroys the whole entity's trust.

Category: **Brand & Expert Positioning** — The person as an entity: positioning statements, speaking and book evidence, media kits and identity-answer repair.

## Inputs

- The full claims list as currently published (gather from all surfaces)
- Certificates, issuer records and links per claim

## Workflow

1. **List every claim live** — Sweep bios, LinkedIn, decks and the site. Claims hide in old decks and organiser bios long after they lapse.
2. **Verify each against the issuer** — Certification valid and current? Award actually given, in that category and year? Record link exists? Each claim gets verified / lapsed / unverifiable.
3. **Rebuild the presentation** — Kept claims are stated as: credential, issuer, year, verify-link. No logos wall, no undated 'award-winning' adjectives.
4. **Remove or qualify the rest** — Lapsed credentials are dated honestly ('held 2019-2023') or removed; unverifiable claims are removed everywhere, decks included.
5. **Calendar the renewals** — Credentials with expiry dates get renewal reminders; an expired credential displayed as current is a live false claim.

## Output

A verified claims register + corrected presentation across surfaces.

## Measurement

100% of published credential claims verify in one click at quarterly audits; zero lapsed-as-current claims.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Never buy or accept pay-to-play 'awards' presented as earned. Engines and journalists both eventually catalogue the mills.
