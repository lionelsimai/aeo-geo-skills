# Agency AEO Delivery Workflow

## What this skill does

The operating system for delivering AEO to clients: a fixed intake (facts, access, approvals), baseline before promises, a monthly rhythm (run, report, ship two priorities), and artefacts stored so any team member can pick up the account. Mirrors the solo-practice version at agency scale.

Category: **GEO Content Ops & Workflows** — Run AEO as an operation: freshness systems, briefs, calendars, QA gates and agency delivery — the workflows that keep citations compounding.

## Inputs

- Client entity facts and access (site, consoles, profiles)
- Approval contacts and turnaround expectations
- The standard artefacts (baseline, tracker, scorecard templates)

## Workflow

1. **Intake with a fact freeze** — Canonical bio/fact sheet signed off by the client before any work. Agency rule: no frozen facts, no programme start.
2. **Baseline before promises** — First deliverable is the measured baseline and realistic tier horizons — in writing, acknowledged by the client.
3. **Run the monthly rhythm** — Week 1: tracker run. Week 2: scorecard + client review. Weeks 2-4: ship the two agreed priorities. Same rhythm every month.
4. **Gate client claims** — Every client-facing fact in content goes through their approval; every agency claim in reports traces to tracker evidence.
5. **Store for handover** — All artefacts in the client folder with the run protocol, so a new account lead reproduces the measurement identically.

## Output

An agency delivery pack per client (intake record, baseline, rhythm calendar, artefacts folder) + the workflow document itself.

## Measurement

On-rhythm months per client (%); baselines preceding any performance claim in 100% of accounts.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Never sell 'guaranteed AI rankings' as an agency line. It is undeliverable, and the churn/refund conversations will quote the sales deck.
