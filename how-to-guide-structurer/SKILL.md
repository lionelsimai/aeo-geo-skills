# How-To Guide Structurer

## What this skill does

Formats instructional content for extraction: a one-line outcome statement, prerequisites and tools listed before step 1, numbered steps that each start with a verb, and a 'common failures' section engines love to cite for troubleshooting prompts.

Category: **Citable Content** — Layer 2 of the strategy: pages built to be lifted — answer-first structure, standalone numbers, FAQ blocks, original data.

## Inputs

- The real process, from someone who has done it
- Tools, costs and time the process genuinely requires

## Workflow

1. **State the outcome first** — 'By the end you will have X.' Then prerequisites, tools, cost and time — all before the steps.
2. **Number verb-first steps** — Each step: one action, the exact setting/value where one exists, and how to know it worked.
3. **Add failure modes** — The 3 most common ways the process breaks, with the fix for each. Troubleshooting sections win citations competitors skip.
4. **Date and version it** — Tools change. Stamp the guide with 'last verified [date]' and re-verify on a schedule.
5. **Close with the checklist** — A compressed checklist version of the steps for repeat users — and for engines summarising the process.

## Output

A publish-ready how-to guide in extraction-friendly structure, with a verification date.

## Measurement

Guide cited or paraphrased in AI answers to its how-to prompts; verification date never older than the set review cycle.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Never publish steps you have not executed. A guide with a wrong step is worse than no guide — in answers, it carries your name.

## Fictional example

Fictional: 'How to claim a Google Knowledge Panel' — 6 steps, each verifiable, with a failure note on unsourced correction suggestions.
