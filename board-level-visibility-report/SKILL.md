# Board-Level Visibility Report

## What this skill does

Converts the monthly scorecards into an executive narrative for boards and clients: the three numbers that matter (mention share on prize prompts, descriptor accuracy, citation sources owned), the commercial meaning, risks (wrong facts circulating), and the next quarter's plan — in language a non-specialist can act on.

Category: **Monitoring & Measurement** — Prove it monthly: frozen prompt sets, citation logs, scorecards and regression alerts — the discipline that separates AEO from superstition.

## Inputs

- Monthly scorecards + benchmark for the period
- Any factual-error incidents found in answers

## Workflow

1. **Open with the answer, literally** — Quote what a major engine currently says to 'best [category] in [place]' and 'Who is [entity]?' — executives grasp AI visibility by reading one answer.
2. **Show the three numbers** — Prize-prompt mention share, descriptor accuracy, % of citations from owned/earned sources — each vs last period and vs baseline.
3. **State the commercial link** — What these prompts are worth (pipeline, bookings, hiring) in the organisation's own terms, without inventing attribution the data cannot support.
4. **Flag factual risks plainly** — Any wrong facts engines state about the organisation, their sources, and the fix status. Boards must hear this from the report, not from a customer.
5. **Close with asks and next steps** — Decisions or resources needed, and the next quarter's two priorities. Every report ends with an action, not a shrug.

## Output

An executive report (answers quoted, three numbers, risks, asks) suitable for board/client distribution.

## Measurement

Report delivered quarterly; every figure matches the underlying scorecards exactly (auditable by anyone who asks).

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Do not inflate with vanity reach numbers or unattributed revenue claims. One caught exaggeration ends executive trust in the whole programme.
