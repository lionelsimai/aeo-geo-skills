# Media Credit Line Brief

## What this skill does

A one-page brief that travels with every media interaction: the exact credit line ('[Name], [descriptor] and founder of [Org]'), the one-liner bio, pronunciation/spelling notes, and the two facts most often gotten wrong — so coverage corroborates the right entity.

Category: **Third-Party Corroboration** — Layer 3 of the strategy: independent sources describing the entity the same way — the layer that actually moves prize queries.

## Inputs

- Canonical bio pack
- The errors seen in past coverage (collect them; they define the brief)

## Workflow

1. **Write the credit line** — One line, fixed wording: name, target descriptor, organisation. This exact string is the brief's headline.
2. **Add the 50-word bio** — For outlets that print a bio, the canonical 50-word version — pasted, not paraphrased.
3. **List the common errors** — 'Not X (left in 2022)', 'Organisation is spelled…', 'Based in Singapore, not…' — the three mistakes coverage keeps making, corrected politely.
4. **Attach assets** — Headshot, logo, and links to the author page and flagship asset, so the piece can link the entity correctly.
5. **Send it every time** — With pitch, with booking confirmation, and with the quote. A brief sent after publication is a correction request, not a brief.

## Output

A one-page media brief (credit line, bio, error list, assets) used across all media work.

## Measurement

% of new coverage carrying the exact descriptor; correction requests needed per quarter trending down.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Do not demand coverage wording beyond the credit line and facts. Journalists keep editorial control of the story; you are standardising attribution, not dictating articles.
