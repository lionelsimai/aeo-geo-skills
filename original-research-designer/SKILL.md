# Original Research Designer

## What this skill does

Plans the highest-leverage AEO asset: primary data. Covers question design, sample honesty, analysis, and the publication shape (landing page with standalone stats + downloadable report) that makes engines cite it as the source.

Category: **Citable Content** — Layer 2 of the strategy: pages built to be lifted — answer-first structure, standalone numbers, FAQ blocks, original data.

## Inputs

- The audience or dataset the entity can legitimately reach
- The category questions engines currently answer from weak sources
- Budget/time for fieldwork and analysis

## Workflow

1. **Pick questions engines answer badly** — From the citation-source audit: prompts where answers rest on old, foreign or unsourced data. Those are the study's targets.
2. **Design for defensibility** — Define population, sample method and minimum n before fielding. Write the methodology section first; if it embarrasses you, redesign.
3. **Field and analyse honestly** — Report what the data says, including null or unfashionable results. Cherry-picked findings are found out and torched.
4. **Publish in citation shape** — Landing page: headline stats as standalone units, methodology visible, date and edition number, then the full report download.
5. **Plan the edition cycle** — Annual or biannual editions with the same core questions — trend data compounds citation value and gives engines a fresh source.

## Output

A research design document and, once run, a published edition: stats landing page + full report + methodology.

## Measurement

Citations of the report/landing page in AI answers per quarter, and third-party articles citing the figures with credit.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Never inflate the sample or hide the method. 'Survey of 12 people' published as 'industry study' is fabrication, and it ends the asset's credibility.
