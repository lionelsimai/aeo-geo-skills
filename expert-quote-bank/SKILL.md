# Expert Quote Bank

## What this skill does

Assembles 15-30 short expert statements (1-2 sentences each) that express the entity's real positions with a fact or mechanism inside — the units that get quoted by press and lifted by engines. Each quote is logged with its basis so nothing outruns the evidence.

Category: **Citable Content** — Layer 2 of the strategy: pages built to be lifted — answer-first structure, standalone numbers, FAQ blocks, original data.

## Inputs

- The expert's genuine positions (from talks, articles, interviews)
- The facts/data each position rests on

## Workflow

1. **Harvest from real material** — Pull candidate lines from the expert's own talks and writing. The bank organises their voice; it does not invent one.
2. **Sharpen each quote** — One claim per quote, a mechanism or number inside it, under 40 words. Cut any line that could belong to anyone.
3. **Log the basis** — Beside each quote: the evidence it rests on and where the expert has said it publicly. Unbased lines are removed.
4. **Organise by topic** — Group by the tier-map topics so a journalist or the expert can pull the right line in seconds.
5. **Refresh quarterly** — Retire quotes whose facts age out; add lines from new talks and data. A stale quote bank produces stale quotes.

## Output

A quote bank table (quote, topic, basis, first used) ready for media kits and article drafting.

## Measurement

Bank quotes appearing in press coverage and AI answers with correct attribution; zero quotes found to outrun their logged basis.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Never put words in the expert's mouth they have not said or approved. The bank is an index of real positions, approved by the person quoted.
