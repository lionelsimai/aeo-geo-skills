# Gemini Visibility Tactics

## What this skill does

Gemini draws deeply on Google's index and entity infrastructure. This skill connects the dots: Knowledge Panel and entity consistency, Google-indexed citable pages, and YouTube/Google surfaces that Gemini surfaces more readily than other engines.

Category: **Platform Surfaces** — Layer 4 of the strategy: each engine retrieves and cites differently — per-engine tactics grounded in observed citation behaviour.

## Inputs

- Entity layer status (consistency sweep, knowledge panel log)
- Google-index status of citable assets

## Workflow

1. **Fix the entity data first** — Gemini leans on structured entity knowledge. Canonical bio consistency, schema and panel accuracy are Gemini tactics, not admin.
2. **Win the index** — Citable pages must be indexed and ranking in Google; Gemini's retrieval starts there.
3. **Use Google-owned surfaces** — YouTube videos with proper titles/descriptions and transcripts, and Google Business/map data where relevant, feed Gemini answers disproportionately.
4. **Check multimodal prompts** — Some Gemini answers pull images/video. Ensure key assets have descriptive titles, captions and alt text in factual wording.
5. **Measure on its own slice** — Gemini answers differ from AI Overviews even on the same query. Track them as separate surfaces in the monthly run.

## Output

A Gemini action list spanning entity data, index fixes and Google-surface updates.

## Measurement

Mention/citation rates on the Gemini slice; entity facts (org, role, place) stated correctly in Gemini identity answers.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Do not treat Gemini and AI Overviews as one surface. Conflating them hides which fix worked.
