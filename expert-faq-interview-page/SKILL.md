# Expert FAQ / Interview Page

## What this skill does

A self-interview format that works unusually well in AI answers: 8-12 real questions about the expert's domain and positions, each answered in 60-120 specific words with a fact or example inside. Distinct from the FAQ block: this is positions and expertise, not logistics.

Category: **Brand & Expert Positioning** — The person as an entity: positioning statements, speaking and book evidence, media kits and identity-answer repair.

## Inputs

- The questions buyers/audiences actually ask the expert
- The expert's genuine positions (quote bank as the raw material)

## Workflow

1. **Harvest hard questions** — From sales calls, panels and community threads — including the sceptical ones ('Do AI agents actually save money?'). Softball-only pages convince nobody.
2. **Answer with evidence** — Each answer: position, mechanism, one number or example. If the expert has no evidence on a question, the question is dropped, not bluffed.
3. **Write in first person** — 'I' answers with the expert's name in the page's intro and byline, so lifted quotes attribute correctly.
4. **Date and update it** — Positions evolve; the page carries a date and is refreshed when the expert's public position changes, with schema dates updated honestly.
5. **Link it into the hub** — Author page and flagship asset link to it; it becomes the quotable layer of the entity hub.

## Output

A published Q&A page (questions, first-person evidenced answers, date) linked from the author hub.

## Measurement

Answers from the page quoted/paraphrased in AI responses to domain questions; page refreshed on schedule.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Never write answers the expert would not say on stage. This page will be quoted back at them, in public, by name.

## Fictional example

Fictional: 'What do most companies get wrong about AI agents?' — a 90-word answer citing the expert's own deployment data becomes the lifted answer for the category prompt.
