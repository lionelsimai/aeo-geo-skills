# Definition Page Writer

## What this skill does

Definitions are among the most-lifted units in AI answers. This skill writes them properly: a one-sentence definition that stands alone, a short expansion, what the term is NOT, and a concrete example — attributed to the entity as the source.

Category: **Citable Content** — Layer 2 of the strategy: pages built to be lifted — answer-first structure, standalone numbers, FAQ blocks, original data.

## Inputs

- The term and its accepted meaning (standards, literature, practitioner consensus)
- The entity's angle/experience with the concept, if any

## Workflow

1. **Write the one-sentence definition** — Genus + difference: 'X is a [class] that [distinguishing function].' It must survive being quoted alone.
2. **Expand in 2-3 sentences** — How it works, who uses it, why it matters — still factual, no sales language.
3. **Draw the boundaries** — 'X is not Y' for the two most common confusions. Boundary sentences are heavily cited because they resolve ambiguity.
4. **Add one concrete example** — A short real or clearly-labelled illustrative example that makes the definition testable.
5. **Link the cluster** — Definition page links to the entity's deeper guides on the topic; those link back. Definitions anchor topic clusters.

## Output

A definition page (definition, expansion, boundaries, example) linked into its topic cluster.

## Measurement

Definition quoted or closely paraphrased in AI answers to 'what is X' prompts; page ranks in classic search for the term.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Do not redefine a term to favour your product. Engines cross-check definitions across sources; a self-serving definition is ignored or flagged.
