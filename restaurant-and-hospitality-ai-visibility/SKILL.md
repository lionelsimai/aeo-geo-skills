# Restaurant & Hospitality AI Visibility

## What this skill does

Hospitality recommendations in AI answers run on facts engines can verify: cuisine, price band, location, hours, signature dishes, amenities — published in text (not only images/PDFs), plus review depth on the platforms travellers actually use.

Category: **Local & Vertical AEO** — The five layers applied per vertical: local businesses, B2B SaaS, ecommerce, professional services and more — same discipline, different surfaces.

## Inputs

- Venue fact sheet (cuisine, price band, address, hours, contact, booking link)
- Current menus in text form
- Review platform status

## Workflow

1. **Publish facts as text** — Menus, price bands and amenities as readable page text. A menu that exists only as an Instagram image does not exist for an engine.
2. **Fix the local base** — GBP/maps/trip platforms aligned to the fact sheet; photos current and honestly representative.
3. **Describe the signature clearly** — The 2-3 things the venue is genuinely known for, stated identically everywhere — engines recommend venues for specific reasons, not general excellence.
4. **Grow real guest reviews** — Natural invitations at the table/receipt; respond to all. Volume and recency on the platforms travellers use are the recommendation fuel.
5. **Track occasion prompts** — 'Best [cuisine] for [occasion] in [area]', 'Where to take clients near [place]' — the prompts that actually fill tables.

## Output

A hospitality visibility pack (text facts live, base aligned, review routine) + occasion prompts tracked.

## Measurement

Recommendation rate on occasion/cuisine prompts; menu/fact accuracy in answers (spot-checked against the real menu).

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Never seed fake guest reviews or stage 'signature' claims the kitchen cannot deliver nightly. Guests fact-check with their mouths and post about it.
