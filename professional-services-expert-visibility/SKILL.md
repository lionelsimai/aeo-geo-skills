# Professional Services Expert Visibility

## What this skill does

Firms are found through people: this skill builds partner/practitioner entities (bios, credentials, insights) inside the firm's entity, with citable insight content and directory/association corroboration — all inside the profession's advertising and claims rules.

Category: **Local & Vertical AEO** — The five layers applied per vertical: local businesses, B2B SaaS, ecommerce, professional services and more — same discipline, different surfaces.

## Inputs

- Practitioner list and their real specialisms/credentials
- The firm's compliance/advertising rules for its jurisdiction and profession
- Existing directory/association profiles (legal/accounting directories, chambers)

## Workflow

1. **Build practitioner entities** — Per partner: canonical bio, verified credentials (award-and-credential-verifier), specialism descriptor, insights byline — firm template, individual facts.
2. **Publish insight content** — Answer-first explainers on client questions ('What happens when…', 'How is X taxed…') authored by named practitioners, reviewed for professional accuracy.
3. **Align directories and associations** — Chambers, law/accounting directories and association profiles carry the same practitioner facts; these are this vertical's corroboration backbone.
4. **Respect the rules absolutely** — No client claims without consent, no outcome guarantees, no misleading specialisation claims — per the profession's code. The code outranks this skill wherever they differ.
5. **Track practitioner prompts** — '[specialism] lawyer/accountant in [place]', 'Who advises on [issue]?' — tracked per practitioner and for the firm.

## Output

Practitioner entity packs + an insights pipeline + directory alignment list, all compliance-checked.

## Measurement

Practitioner mention rate on specialism prompts; 100% of public claims pass the firm's compliance review.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Never invent client outcomes or imply specialisation accreditation the practitioner does not hold. In regulated professions that is misconduct, not marketing.
