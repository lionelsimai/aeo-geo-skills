# Book as Entity Evidence

## What this skill does

Treats an author's books as structured proof: each title registered consistently (ISBN, publisher, date), described identically across surfaces, reviewed on legitimate platforms, and woven into schema and the author page so engines count them as works of the person entity.

Category: **Brand & Expert Positioning** — The person as an entity: positioning statements, speaking and book evidence, media kits and identity-answer repair.

## Inputs

- Full book list with ISBNs and publisher details
- Book-surface register (from book-surface-optimiser)

## Workflow

1. **Register the facts once** — Per book: exact title/subtitle, ISBN-13, publisher, publication date, editions. One record; every surface copies it.
2. **Align descriptions everywhere** — Retailer, publisher, Goodreads and own-site descriptions tell the same story about the book and name the author with the descriptor.
3. **Earn legitimate reviews** — Reviews from real readers via normal reading channels. No review swaps, no purchased reviews — the same absolute rule as all review platforms.
4. **Wire the works into schema** — Person item's author-of links each Book item with its ISBN identifier, so the graph states 'this person wrote these catalogued works'.
5. **Use books in pitches** — Roundup and podcast pitches cite the books as evidence with their catalogue links — verifiable in one click by editors.

## Output

A works register (per-book facts + surface status) and schema/pitch integration notes.

## Measurement

Identity answers list the books correctly with the descriptor; zero fact conflicts across book surfaces.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: 'Bestseller' claims need the list, category and date that prove them. Unprovable superlatives are removed from every surface.
