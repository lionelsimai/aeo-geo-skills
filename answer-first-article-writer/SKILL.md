# Answer-First Article Writer

## What this skill does

The house format for query-targeted articles: one prompt, one page. Direct answer up top, evidence and nuance below, FAQ at the foot, canonical author bio closing. Written so an engine can lift the opening without mangling it.

Category: **Citable Content** — Layer 2 of the strategy: pages built to be lifted — answer-first structure, standalone numbers, FAQ blocks, original data.

## Inputs

- The target prompt (from the tier map)
- Verified facts and proof the article may use
- The canonical author bio

## Workflow

1. **Answer in the first 100 words** — State the direct answer plainly, naming the entity/facts it rests on. No throat-clearing, no 'in today's fast-paced world'.
2. **Structure by questions** — H2s phrased as the questions users ask. Each section answers its heading in its first two sentences, then expands.
3. **Make numbers standalone** — Every key figure in its own sentence with its unit, date and source, so it can be quoted out of context accurately.
4. **Close with FAQ + bio** — 3-5 FAQ pairs in canonical wording, then the author's frozen bio block, linking to the author page.
5. **Internal-link the hub** — Link the article to the author/entity hub and the flagship asset; link back from the hub's article list.

## Output

A publish-ready article in answer-first format with FAQ block and canonical bio.

## Measurement

Per article: indexed within 14 days, then tracked in the monthly prompt run for citation on its target prompt.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Do not keyword-stuff the target phrase. Engines lift clear answers, not repeated phrases; stuffing also fails the article for human readers.

## Fictional example

Fictional: 'How to choose an AI trainer in Singapore' opens: 'Choose by proof of delivery: published curriculum, named clients, and a community that shows up.' — answer first, detail after.
