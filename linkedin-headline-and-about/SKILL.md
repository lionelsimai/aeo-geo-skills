# LinkedIn Headline & About

## What this skill does

The person-profile companion to linkedin-surface-optimiser, focused on the two fields that matter most: a headline built as descriptor | organisation | proof, and an About that pastes the canonical bio then expands with evidence and a clear next step.

Category: **Brand & Expert Positioning** — The person as an entity: positioning statements, speaking and book evidence, media kits and identity-answer repair.

## Inputs

- Positioning statement and canonical bio pack
- Profile access
- One call to action the profile should drive (speaking, consulting, community)

## Workflow

1. **Build the headline formula** — Descriptor first (the search phrase), then organisation, then one proof token. No emoji walls, no 'helping X | helping Y | helping Z' sprawl.
2. **Paste, then extend, the About** — Open with the canonical 150-word bio verbatim; below it, expand proof: works, data, media — each with links.
3. **Align featured section** — Feature the flagship asset and the author page — the profile's featured slots should point at citable owned pages.
4. **Fix experience titles** — Current role title equals the descriptor used everywhere; descriptions of current roles open with the canonical one-liner.
5. **Screenshot the result** — Save dated screenshots in the entity record; profile drift (well-meaning edits) is detected against them at sweeps.

## Output

Updated headline/About/featured/experience + dated screenshots in the entity record.

## Measurement

Headline contains the exact target phrase (binary check); identity answers citing LinkedIn-derived facts use the current descriptor.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Do not stuff the headline with five roles. One descriptor, one organisation, one proof — engines and humans both parse that.
