# Partner Co-Authored Content

## What this skill does

Content published with a credible partner (association, platform, media outlet, research firm) carries corroboration neither party can self-publish: their domain, their audience, your joint byline. This skill designs and runs those collaborations cleanly.

Category: **Third-Party Corroboration** — Layer 3 of the strategy: independent sources describing the entity the same way — the layer that actually moves prize queries.

## Inputs

- A partner with real audience overlap and editorial standards
- A topic where both parties bring genuine data or expertise

## Workflow

1. **Choose the partner for credibility** — Their domain authority and audience trust are the asset. A partner chosen only for a link is a link scheme, not a collaboration.
2. **Agree the contribution split** — Who brings data, who writes, who publishes, whose canonical bios appear. Written down before work starts.
3. **Publish on the strongest surface** — Usually the partner's or a neutral publication's domain, with both entities' canonical descriptors in bylines.
4. **Cross-link honestly** — Each party links from its own hub to the piece with natural anchor text. No hidden or paid link arrangements.
5. **Log it as corroboration** — The piece enters the corroboration register and the citation-gap tracker watches whether engines pick it up.

## Output

A co-authored piece live on the partner/third-party surface + register entries for both entities.

## Measurement

Citations of the joint piece in AI answers; partner pieces per year (2-4 quality collaborations beat 20 swaps).

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Guest-post exchanges at scale ('you post mine, I post yours') are a known spam pattern. Collaborations must be substantive and disclosed.

## Fictional example

Fictional: a community organisation and a research firm co-publish an adoption study; engines cite the firm's domain and name both entities in category answers.
