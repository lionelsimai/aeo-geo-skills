# Byline & Author Bio System

## What this skill does

Contributed articles, guest posts and syndicated pieces each carry a byline — and each byline is written fresh (and differently) without a system. This skill creates the byline template, the rules per outlet type, and a register of placements to sweep for drift.

Category: **Brand & Expert Positioning** — The person as an entity: positioning statements, speaking and book evidence, media kits and identity-answer repair.

## Inputs

- Canonical bio pack
- The outlet list where the expert contributes

## Workflow

1. **Create the byline template** — Two fixed versions: one-line byline (name + descriptor + org) and a 2-3 sentence end-of-article bio with the author-page link.
2. **Set the rules per outlet** — Where the outlet allows links: author page link mandatory. Where it controls format: descriptor is non-negotiable, links negotiable.
3. **Register every placement** — Each published piece logged with its printed byline text. The register is what the consistency sweep checks third-party bios against.
4. **Fix drift at republication** — Syndicated copies often carry the oldest bio. Each republication is a chance to supply the current byline — take it.
5. **Brief editors once** — A standing note to regular outlets with the template attached: 'please use this byline for all pieces.' Most comply gladly.

## Output

A byline template pack + a placements register maintained per publication.

## Measurement

% of new placements using the template byline (target 100%); register matches live pages at sweeps.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Do not let a ghostwriter 'vary' bylines for style. In entity terms, variation is corruption.
