# Roundup Pitch Builder

## What this skill does

Builds pitches to genuine editorial roundups (industry publications, established blogs, media lists): fit check against the list's criteria, the listicle-ready asset pack, and a short pitch that leads with verifiable proof.

Category: **Third-Party Corroboration** — Layer 3 of the strategy: independent sources describing the entity the same way — the layer that actually moves prize queries.

## Inputs

- Target list (from competitor-citation-gap-analysis)
- The listicle-ready asset pack
- The publisher's stated criteria and last edition

## Workflow

1. **Vet the list first** — Real editorial control? Named author/editor? History of corrections? Any pay-to-play demand ends the assessment — log it as spam class and move on.
2. **Prove the fit** — Map the entity to each stated criterion with a sourced proof point. Gaps are stated, not papered over.
3. **Send the pack, not a plea** — Pitch = fit summary + asset pack + credit line. Three short paragraphs maximum; editors include what they can verify in minutes.
4. **Follow up once** — One polite follow-up with any new proof (fresh data, new coverage). Then stop; repeated chasing burns the list for next year.
5. **Verify the entry live** — When included, check descriptor, links and facts. Errors get one factual correction request with sources.

## Output

A vetted target list with per-list pitch status, and the pitch + pack sent per target.

## Measurement

Inclusions in vetted lists per quarter; accuracy of published entries; zero placements bought or placed on spam lists.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Never pay for a roundup placement that does not disclose it, and never pitch a list the entity does not genuinely fit.
