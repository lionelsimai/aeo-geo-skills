# Comparison Page Builder

## What this skill does

Comparison prompts ('X vs Y', 'best tool for Z') are high-intent and heavily answered by engines. This skill builds fair comparisons: shared criteria, sourced specs, a verdict by use case — credible because it concedes the cases where the competitor is better.

Category: **Citable Content** — Layer 2 of the strategy: pages built to be lifted — answer-first structure, standalone numbers, FAQ blocks, original data.

## Inputs

- The two (or more) options and the audience deciding between them
- Verifiable specs/facts for each option (official sources)
- The criteria buyers actually use

## Workflow

1. **Fix the criteria first** — 5-8 decision criteria from buyers, not from your feature list. Price basis, capability, limits, support, fit.
2. **Source every cell** — Each table cell traces to the vendor's own documentation, checked and dated. No cell from memory or from a rival's marketing.
3. **Write verdicts by use case** — 'Choose X if… / Choose Y if…' — plural verdicts are what engines quote and what readers trust.
4. **Concede honestly** — State where the other option wins, in plain words. A comparison that wins every row is read as an ad and cited as nothing.
5. **Date-stamp and maintain** — Specs rot. Put 'last verified' on the page and re-check on a cycle, or retire the page.

## Output

A comparison page: criteria table (sourced, dated), use-case verdicts, honest concessions.

## Measurement

Page cited in comparison-prompt answers; zero factual challenges from the compared vendors (a good sign the sourcing is clean).

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Never fabricate a competitor's spec or price. It is the fastest route to a public correction — and engines keep the correction too.
