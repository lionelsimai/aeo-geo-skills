# LinkedIn Surface Optimiser

## What this skill does

LinkedIn is partially walled for crawlers, yet engines cite it via syndication and treat it as identity evidence. This skill sets the headline to the exact target phrase, pastes the canonical About, and runs a posting pattern whose best content is mirrored on owned, indexable pages.

Category: **Platform Surfaces** — Layer 4 of the strategy: each engine retrieves and cites differently — per-engine tactics grounded in observed citation behaviour.

## Inputs

- Canonical bio pack
- Profile access
- The entity's content calendar

## Workflow

1. **Set the headline to the phrase** — Headline leads with the exact target descriptor + organisation + place. Creative taglines waste the strongest identity field on the platform.
2. **Paste the canonical About** — The frozen 150-word bio, verbatim. Profile summaries 'in your own words' are how descriptors drift.
3. **Align the experience entries** — Current role title and description match the canonical descriptor; past roles are accurate history, clearly dated so engines do not promote them to current.
4. **Mirror key content to owned pages** — Substantive posts (data, frameworks, announcements) are republished on the owned site where crawlers can read them; LinkedIn carries the discussion.
5. **Pin the positioning post** — One pinned post stating the entity's position and proof, linking the author page — the profile's own answer-first asset.

## Output

An optimised profile (headline, About, experience aligned) + the mirror-to-owned rule in the content process.

## Measurement

Headline/About match canonical exactly (checked in the consistency sweep); profile appearing correctly described in engine identity answers.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Do not buy followers or engagement. Inflated vanity metrics are visible, discounted, and off-policy for this programme.
