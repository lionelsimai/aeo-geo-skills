# Answer Engine Landscape Scan

## What this skill does

A short, evidence-based scan of the answer-engine landscape for one market: which engines the target audience uses, which indexes they retrieve from, and how each one's citation behaviour differs.

Category: **AEO/GEO Strategy & Audits** — Know where the brand stands in AI answers before spending anything: audits, baselines, share of voice and roadmaps.

## Inputs

- Target audience and geography
- Query types that matter (local, B2B research, consumer, how-to)
- Any analytics on current referral sources

## Workflow

1. **List the candidate engines** — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews / AI Mode, Copilot, Claude — plus regional or vertical engines if the market has them.
2. **Map retrieval sources** — Per engine: Bing index, Google index, own crawl, or partner data. This decides where the technical floor work goes (e.g. Copilot citations follow Bing).
3. **Map citation behaviour** — Note per engine: cites owned pages? prefers roundups? uses transcripts? shows sources at all? Verify with three sample prompts each.
4. **Rank for this audience** — Rank engines by audience fit, not global hype. A B2B APAC audience and a US consumer audience produce different rankings.
5. **Write the scan** — One page: ranking, why, and what each top engine demands. Revisit every 6 months or on a major engine launch.

## Output

A one-page landscape scan with an engine ranking and per-engine implications for the programme.

## Measurement

Decision quality: the programme's engine list and effort split match the scan. Re-scan on schedule; log engine product changes that alter citation behaviour.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Do not optimise equally for every engine by default. Effort follows the audience, and the scan is what proves the split.

## Fictional example

Fictional: for a Singapore professional-services firm, the scan ranks Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT first (audience behaviour), Perplexity third (research-heavy partners), Copilot fourth via Bing.
