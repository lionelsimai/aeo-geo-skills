# Reputation Answer Repair

## What this skill does

The Tier 3 repair programme: identity answers built from stale coverage (an old employer, an abandoned field) are fixed at their sources — updated bios, corrected pages, and enough current canonical signal that engines re-lead with the present-day entity.

Category: **Brand & Expert Positioning** — The person as an entity: positioning statements, speaking and book evidence, media kits and identity-answer repair.

## Inputs

- Saved 'Who is X?' answers across engines (the wrong text, verbatim)
- Entity-resolution diagnostic (which sources feed the wrong claims)

## Workflow

1. **Freeze the wrong answer** — Save current identity answers in full. Repair is measured against this text, claim by claim.
2. **Rank the feeding sources** — From the diagnostic: which pages supply the stale lead facts, ordered by authority and traffic.
3. **Fix owned sources completely** — Homepage, author page, profiles — 100% canonical. A repair that leaves the homepage contradicting the target is theatre.
4. **Correct or out-publish third parties** — Correction requests with canonical text for fixable pages; for unfixable ones, publish stronger current sources (press, event pages, book surfaces) that outrank them.
5. **Verify monthly, claim by claim** — Re-run identity prompts; mark each stale claim present/absent. Repair is done when the lead facts are canonical on all tracked engines for two consecutive runs.

## Output

A repair tracker (claim, source, fix applied, monthly status) through to closure.

## Measurement

Stale lead claims count trends to zero; 'Who is X?' opens with canonical descriptor and current organisation on all tracked engines.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Do not flood the web with low-quality 'profile' pages to bury the stale result. Spam profiles are discounted and can become the new problem.
