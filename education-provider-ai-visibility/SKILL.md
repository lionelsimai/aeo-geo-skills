# Education Provider AI Visibility

## What this skill does

For education and training providers: programme facts (curriculum, duration, format, accreditation, funding eligibility) published citable-clean, instructor entities built, and learner outcomes reported only with real cohort data and consent.

Category: **Local & Vertical AEO** — The five layers applied per vertical: local businesses, B2B SaaS, ecommerce, professional services and more — same discipline, different surfaces.

## Inputs

- Programme catalogue with accreditation/funding facts
- Instructor bios and credentials
- Outcome data that genuinely exists (completion, placement — with method)

## Workflow

1. **Publish programme facts precisely** — Per programme: who it is for, curriculum, duration, format, price/funding, accreditation status — each fact on its own page, dated.
2. **Build instructor entities** — Named instructors with canonical bios and verified credentials; 'learn from practitioners' only where practitioners genuinely teach.
3. **State outcomes with method** — Any outcome figure carries its cohort, period and method, or it is not published. Prospective students and engines deserve the same honesty.
4. **Answer the decider questions** — FAQ/guides for 'Is [course] worth it?', '[Funding] eligibility', 'X vs Y course' — the prompts enrolment decisions start from.
5. **Track category prompts** — 'Best [skill] course in [place]', '[certification] training provider' — per programme family, monthly.

## Output

An education visibility pack (programme pages, instructor entities, outcomes policy) + prompt tracking per programme.

## Measurement

Citation/recommendation rate per programme family; outcome claims audit: 100% carry method or are removed.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Never publish placement or salary outcomes you cannot evidence cohort-by-cohort. Education regulators and engines both punish invented outcomes.
