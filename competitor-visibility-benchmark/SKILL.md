# Competitor Visibility Benchmark

## What this skill does

Extends share-of-voice into a full benchmark: per competitor, the same scores the entity gets (mention rate, citation rate, position, descriptor quality) plus what they shipped recently that plausibly moved their numbers. Benchmarks inform strategy; they are not league-table theatre.

Category: **Monitoring & Measurement** — Prove it monthly: frozen prompt sets, citation logs, scorecards and regression alerts — the discipline that separates AEO from superstition.

## Inputs

- The monthly run data (entity + competitors scored identically)
- Light monitoring of competitors' public moves (content, coverage, events)

## Workflow

1. **Score identically** — Same prompts, same rubric, same run. A benchmark with different methods per entity is marketing fiction.
2. **Show trajectories, not snapshots** — Two+ runs minimum before any 'overtaking' language. Single-run rankings are noise dressed as insight.
3. **Annotate their moves** — For any competitor jump: what did they publish/get cited in during the window? Cause hypotheses labelled as such.
4. **Find the exploitable gap** — Where is a competitor strong on a tier the entity has not contested, and is it worth contesting? Not every gap deserves budget.
5. **Write the so-what** — Close with the 2 strategic implications for next quarter. A benchmark without implications is a screenshotting exercise.

## Output

A benchmark report (score table, trajectories, competitor moves, implications).

## Measurement

Benchmark produced quarterly from identical-method runs; implications visibly acted on or explicitly rejected with reasons.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Never benchmark against entities in a different league and call it motivation. The set is the 3-5 names engines actually group you with.
