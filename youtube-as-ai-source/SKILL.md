# YouTube as an AI Source

## What this skill does

Engines retrieve video transcripts as text. This skill turns a channel into an entity surface: titles carrying real query phrases, descriptions leading with the canonical bio, transcripts present and accurate, and talk videos titled so category prompts can find them.

Category: **Platform Surfaces** — Layer 4 of the strategy: each engine retrieves and cites differently — per-engine tactics grounded in observed citation behaviour.

## Inputs

- Channel access
- Canonical bio pack
- The talk/video back catalogue

## Workflow

1. **Fix the channel About first** — Canonical bio pasted in full — the channel itself is an entity surface, not just a video host.
2. **Retitle by query** — Talk titles carry the topic phrase + speaker + organisation ('Agentic AI in Singapore — [Name], [Org]'), not insider event names.
3. **Write descriptions properly** — First lines: what the video proves/covers with the canonical descriptor; then chapters, links to the author page and flagship asset.
4. **Ensure transcripts exist** — Auto-captions checked for the entity's name/descriptor accuracy; upload corrected transcripts for key talks — engines read this text.
5. **Repurpose the best talks** — Each strong talk becomes an owned article (the talk is the draft), linking the video; article and video corroborate each other.

## Output

A channel fix list applied (About, titles, descriptions, transcripts) + a per-talk repurposing queue.

## Measurement

Videos surfacing in AI answers / video results for topic prompts; transcript name-accuracy 100% on key talks.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Do not keyword-stuff titles into clickbait. The title is metadata for retrieval, and a misleading title costs the channel's credibility with humans too.
