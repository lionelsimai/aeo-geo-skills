# AEO/GEO Readiness Scorecard

## What this skill does

A fast diagnostic scorecard: 25 checks across the five layers, each scored present / partial / missing from evidence, producing a readiness grade and the two layers to fix first.

Category: **AEO/GEO Strategy & Audits** — Know where the brand stands in AI answers before spending anything: audits, baselines, share of voice and roadmaps.

## Inputs

- Entity website and main profiles
- Any existing audit or baseline
- Access to view (not edit) the site's schema and robots.txt

## Workflow

1. **Score the entity layer** — Canonical bio exists? Same descriptor on site, LinkedIn, and top profiles? Homepage carries the target phrase?
2. **Score content and corroboration** — Answer-first flagship asset? Query-targeted articles? Independent roundups/press/podcasts naming the entity with the same descriptor?
3. **Score surfaces and technical** — YouTube/Substack/newsletter surfaces carry the bio? Person/Org schema valid? llms.txt present? AI crawlers allowed? GSC + Bing verified?
4. **Grade honestly** — Readiness = checks passed / 25, shown per layer, never as one blended number that hides a zero layer.
5. **Pick two fixes** — Recommend the two lowest layers with the highest leverage, with the specific first action for each.

## Output

A one-page scorecard (five layer scores + evidence notes) and the two priority fixes.

## Measurement

Use as the week-1 diagnostic and re-score at day 90. A layer at zero caps the whole programme no matter how strong the others are.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Score from evidence you can link to, not from what the client says exists. 'We have schema' is checked, not believed.
