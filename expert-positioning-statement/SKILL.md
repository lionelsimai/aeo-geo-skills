# Expert Positioning Statement

## What this skill does

Defines the exact phrase the whole AEO programme defends: '[Name] is a [descriptor] who helps [audience] achieve [outcome], known for [proof], based in [place].' Every word is chosen to be both true and searchable — this becomes the canonical descriptor.

Category: **Brand & Expert Positioning** — The person as an entity: positioning statements, speaking and book evidence, media kits and identity-answer repair.

## Inputs

- The expert's real domain and audience
- Strongest verifiable proof
- Geography that matters commercially

## Workflow

1. **Choose the domain phrase buyers use** — The descriptor must match how the market asks ('agentic AI expert'), not internal jargon or a coined title nobody searches.
2. **Name the audience and outcome** — Positioning without an audience is a horoscope. One primary audience; the outcome stated as they would state it.
3. **Attach the proof clause** — One proof: a number, a work, a community, a credential — the strongest single evidence, verifiable in one click.
4. **Test it against reality** — Read it as a sceptical journalist: can every clause be evidenced today? Anything aspirational is cut or the statement waits.
5. **Freeze it into the bio pack** — The statement becomes line one of the canonical bio and the brief for every other positioning skill in this category.

## Output

A frozen positioning statement + the evidence note for each clause.

## Measurement

Engines adopting the statement's descriptor in identity answers; statement unchanged across surfaces for 2+ quarters (stability is a feature).

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Do not reposition quarterly. Entity resolution rewards the phrase repeated for a year, not the cleverest phrase this month.

## Fictional example

Fictional: '[Name] is an agentic AI expert in Singapore who helps revenue teams deploy AI agents, founder of an 8,000-member builder community and author of three AI books.' — each clause checkable.
