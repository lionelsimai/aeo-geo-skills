# Ecommerce Product AI Visibility

## What this skill does

For stores and brands: product data engines can use (clean titles, specs, structured data), genuine review depth, buying-guide content that answers 'best X for Y' honestly, and marketplace/retailer surfaces aligned with the brand's own facts.

Category: **Local & Vertical AEO** — The five layers applied per vertical: local businesses, B2B SaaS, ecommerce, professional services and more — same discipline, different surfaces.

## Inputs

- Product catalogue and its current data quality
- Review status per channel
- The category prompts customers ask

## Workflow

1. **Clean the product facts** — Titles, specs, materials, sizes, compatibility — stated completely and identically on the store, marketplaces and feeds. Engines recommend products whose facts they can verify.
2. **Add Product schema with real data** — Product/Offer/Review markup with true prices, availability and aggregate ratings that match the live page (schema-validation-qa rules apply).
3. **Build buying-guide content** — 'Best [product] for [use case]' guides that include competitors fairly — the pages engines lift for recommendation prompts.
4. **Grow genuine reviews** — Post-purchase review invitations to real buyers on the store and marketplaces; review depth is a primary product-recommendation signal.
5. **Track product prompts** — Monthly: category, comparison and 'for [use case]' prompts, logging which products/brands get recommended and why (price? reviews? specs?).

## Output

A product-visibility pack (data fixes, schema status, guide plan, review process) + product prompts in the tracker.

## Measurement

Recommendation rate for tracked product prompts; product facts consistent across store/marketplace/feed at spot checks.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Never mark up ratings or prices the page does not show, and never fake product reviews. Commerce spam is penalised by platforms, engines and regulators alike.
