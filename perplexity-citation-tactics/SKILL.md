# Perplexity Citation Tactics

## What this skill does

Perplexity behaves like a research assistant: it cites numerously, favours current and primary sources, and shows its working. This skill aligns publishing freshness, source transparency and primary-data assets to that behaviour.

Category: **Platform Surfaces** — Layer 4 of the strategy: each engine retrieves and cites differently — per-engine tactics grounded in observed citation behaviour.

## Inputs

- Perplexity slice of the audit (answers + full citation lists)
- The entity's primary-data and flagship assets

## Workflow

1. **Study its citation lists** — Perplexity exposes sources per answer — count which types dominate for your prompts (news, primary data, reference, community).
2. **Prioritise primary sources** — Original data and official pages do disproportionately well. Put the flagship data page and its stats in citation shape first.
3. **Keep dates honest and current** — Perplexity weights recency for newsy prompts. Genuinely maintained pages (real updates, real dates) beat static ones.
4. **Make sourcing transparent** — Pages that cite their own sources clearly are easier for a research engine to trust and re-cite. Add source lines to stats and claims.
5. **Track citation position** — Being source #2 vs #14 matters for click-through and prominence; log positions, not just presence.

## Output

A Perplexity action list (assets to prioritise, freshness fixes) + citation-position tracking in the monthly log.

## Measurement

Citation rate and median citation position on the Perplexity slice of the tracker.

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: Do not spam Perplexity with low-value pages hoping for citation volume. Its lists are curated by relevance; thin pages simply never appear.
