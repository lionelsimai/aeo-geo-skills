# Listicle-Ready Asset Builder

## What this skill does

Legitimate roundups include entities they can verify fast. This skill builds the editor-ready pack: canonical one-liner, category fit statement, 3 proof points with sources, logo/headshot, and the correct credit line — everything a roundup or directory editor needs, nothing spun.

Category: **Citable Content** — Layer 2 of the strategy: pages built to be lifted — answer-first structure, standalone numbers, FAQ blocks, original data.

## Inputs

- Canonical bio pack and stat bank
- The target roundup's category and criteria (read them first)
- Brand assets (logo, headshot) at publication quality

## Workflow

1. **Read the roundup's criteria** — Most legit lists state who qualifies. If the entity does not fit, do not submit — a mismatched pitch burns the editor relationship.
2. **Assemble the pack** — One-pager: one-liner, why it fits this category (with evidence), 3 sourced proof points, asset links, contact.
3. **Write the credit line** — The exact line to print: name, descriptor, organisation, place, URL. Editors copy what you give them.
4. **Pitch factually** — The pitch email states fit and offers verification. No superlatives the editor would have to take on faith.
5. **Track and thank** — Log submissions and outcomes; when included, verify the printed facts and thank the editor with the correction-free link.

## Output

An editor-ready pack + a submission tracker for legitimate roundups and directories.

## Measurement

Inclusion rate on fitted targets, and factual accuracy of published entries (target: zero corrections needed).

## Guardrails

- **No fake citations.** Only cite sources that exist and say what you claim they say. Never invent a mention, a quote, a ranking or a review.
- **No fabricated stats.** Every number comes from the user's own data or a named public source. If a figure cannot be verified, mark it `[verify]` and leave it out of published copy.
- **White-hat only.** No spam directories, no paid listicle placements on SEO-spam roundups, no cloaking, no hidden text, no fake reviews, no doorway pages.
- **No guarantees.** AI answer placement cannot be guaranteed. State realistic ranges and timelines; engines change without notice.
- **Entity facts stay consistent.** Use the canonical bio and descriptor verbatim wherever the entity is described; flag any surface that contradicts it.
- Skill-specific: This skill is for legitimate editorial lists only. Paid-placement spam roundups are excluded by policy, however easy they are.
